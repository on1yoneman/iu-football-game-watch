What we’ve learned

through Northwestern
Bell earned the start and nearly went wire-to-wire. Jaylen Bell started opposite Jamari Sharpe and played 59 of 60 defensive snaps. The Daily Hoosier reported he allowed no catches on two targets; Ryland Gandy did not log a defensive snap. Bell is now the effective starter based on current usage, while A.J. Harris remains on the underlying depth chart with an availability tag.
Daley made an immediate impact after eligibility was restored. Stephen Daley played 32 snaps and produced seven tackles, a sack, a tackle for loss, four pressures and two forced fumbles. Kellan Wyatt returned for 25 snaps. Their eligibility is distinct from injury status.
The front generated pressure but missed finishes. IU produced 19 pressures and four sacks. Mario Landino had a sack; Tobi Osunsanmi had a sack and two tackles for loss. Cignetti and players emphasized missed tackles on Aidan Chiles as the main second-half issue.
Hardy left late; Utzinger is the two-LB backup. Rolijah Hardy had eight tackles before leaving with an injury. Jeff Utzinger replaced him and played 14 snaps. Hardy is probable on the Sept. 30 Rutgers report and remains the underlying starter.
Four-linebacker package appeared again. True freshman Jacob Savage started in the package but played only two snaps. Kaiden Turner played 19; the package is situational, not evidence that Savage has displaced the regular two-LB starters.

Offense · players to watch

#12
Dylan Lonergan · QB

The Boston College transfer is the primary quarterback. He threw for 252 yards and three TDs against USC, then 214 yards and two TDs against Howard. Turnovers are the pressure point: five interceptions through four games.

#3
Antwan Raymond · RB

Rutgers' engine. He has three straight 100-yard games and ran for 140 yards and three TDs against Howard, pushing him over 2,000 career rushing yards.

#8
KJ Duff · WR

The premier matchup outside. He had 131 yards against Howard, topped 2,000 career receiving yards and has caught a touchdown in five straight games.

#2
Ben Black III · WR

Starting receiver opposite Duff. He caught a 16-yard touchdown against Howard and gives Lonergan another vertical option.

Defense · players to watch

#5
Bradlee Jones · DB

Starting corner who broke out with two interceptions against Howard. He also forced a fumble at Boston College.

#2
Moses Walker · LB

Middle-of-the-defense playmaker. He had five tackles, 2.5 tackles for loss and a half sack in the Howard win.

#96
Keshon Griffin · DL

Interior disruptor who produced a strip sack against Howard. Rutgers had a season-high four sacks in that game.

#42
DJ Allen · DL

Front-line defender who recovered Griffin's forced fumble against Howard and is part of the pressure package IU's line must handle.

What Rutgers wants to do

  • Feed #3 Raymond. Kirk Ciarrocca's offense is built to establish the run and keep the quarterback on schedule.
  • Find #8 Duff downfield. Duff is the proven contested-catch and explosive receiving threat; IU's corners cannot lose him.
  • Control possession. Rutgers averages just over 32 minutes of possession and converts 43.5% on third down.
  • Create pressure without selling out. The defense posted four sacks against Howard after struggling through the 0–3 start.

What to watch Saturday

  • IU's first road game. This is Homecoming and a Blackout night at SHI Stadium after four straight home games for Indiana.
  • Make Lonergan protect the ball. #12 has shown real arm talent, but Rutgers has thrown nine interceptions as a team through four games.
  • Backfield availability. Richard is probable and Cuono questionable on the Sept. 30 report. Beebe could still carry a large share; Martin is out.
  • Do not judge Rutgers by 1–3 alone. The Scarlet Knights scored 35 on USC and just put up 534 yards and 58 points against Howard.

through Northwestern
Beebe carried the backfield after Richard went down. Lee Beebe Jr. played 59 of 74 offensive snaps and ran 24 times for 203 yards and a touchdown. Turbo Richard had 68 yards on nine carries before leaving hurt. The Sept. 30 Rutgers report lists him probable; Richard remains in the lead rotation.
Slot work is shifting toward Chandler. The Daily Hoosier noted Davion Chandler is at least splitting the slot with Tyler Morris and may be gaining ground. Against Northwestern both played 20+ snaps; Chandler had two catches for 20 yards.
Barker has separated at tight end. Andrew Barker played 51 snaps, Trevor Gibbs 26 and Brock Schott only six. Barker caught his first career pass; Gibbs has moved ahead of Schott in the current rotation.
Becker and Marsh remain the clear top outside targets. Becker played 69 snaps and caught 7 for 98 yards. Marsh played 68 and caught 5 for 52 yards and IU's only passing touchdown.
Line played the full game, but right tackle is a focus. All five starting linemen played all 74 offensive snaps. Cignetti specifically said right-tackle play needs to improve after Adedamola Ajani allowed the two pressures charged to the line.
